About Pramod

Pramod Begur Nagaraj is a cloud and AI infrastructure architect, independent researcher, and open-source technologist specializing in Agentic AI, AIOps, observability, DevOps, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), Chaos Engineering, and resilient distributed systems. Recognized as a Microsoft MVP and Alibaba Cloud MVP, he brings extensive expertise in designing and operating scalable, reliable, and production-ready cloud and AI systems.

As a Co-Founder and Lead Architect of DeepAgentLabs, he focuses on architecting open-source AgenticOps infrastructure, advancing the observability, reliability, resilience, and governance of autonomous AI systems in production environments.

Beyond open-source engineering, Pramod contributes to IEEE standards development and IEEE-USA technology policy initiatives, helping advance industry practices for AI operations, observability, and responsible deployment. A multi-cloud architect with 24 cloud certifications, he also serves as Chair of ACM Dallas SIGAI, supporting technical innovation, research collaboration, and developer communities.

His work bridges cloud-native engineering, Agentic AI architecture, open-source innovation, and technical standardization, contributing to the operational foundations of trustworthy, scalable, and resilient AI-native systems.

About Nithesh

Nithesh Gudipuri is an enterprise technology and Artificial Intelligence leader specializing in financial-services architecture, distributed systems, enterprise modernization, and agentic AI. He serves as Associate Director, Technology at Raymond James, where his work spans the architecture and modernization of Core Enterprise Business Services supporting business consumers across financial-services, together with mission-critical regulatory and operations technology.

He is also a Co-Founder of Deep Agent Labs, where his work focuses on agent observability, behavioral evaluation, semantic continuity, intelligent orchestration, and the development of governed and resilient agentic systems for enterprise environments.

His work includes Neural Modernization for HPE NonStop Systems (NM4N), an AI-assisted methodology for reconstructing the behavior, dependencies, and technical knowledge embedded within mission-critical legacy systems to enable modernization while preserving behavioral continuity. His broader work explores how established enterprise architecture and distributed-systems principles can provide the foundation for trustworthy autonomous systems.

Nithesh is an IEEE Senior Member, SCRS Distinguished Fellow and Founding Chair of the ACM Tampa Bay Professional Chapter and contributes to the broader technology community through research, technical publications, invited talks, mentoring, and professional service.
